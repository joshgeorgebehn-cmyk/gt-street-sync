# Shipping policy

## Where we deliver
We deliver Australia wide. We do not currently ship overseas.

## Delivery costs
Standard delivery is free Australia wide on every order of A$1,000 or more, with no remote area surcharge. Below A$1,000, standard delivery is A$19.95 for most items and A$80 for exhaust systems. The full delivery cost is shown before you pay, and every price includes GST.

## Processing times
In stock orders are usually dispatched within 1 to 3 business days of payment. Orders placed after hours or on weekends and public holidays are processed the next business day.

Some brands are in high demand and some products ship directly from the manufacturer or distributor. Where an item is not in stock, or timing is longer than usual, we will contact you with an expected timeframe before dispatch, and you can cancel for a full refund if the new timing does not suit you.

## Delivery times
Delivery estimates are shown at checkout and depend on the courier and your location. Regional and remote areas can take longer. We cannot guarantee a delivery date, but we will help chase any delay with the courier.

## Tracking
We email tracking details when your order is dispatched. Courier tracking can take up to 24 hours to update.

## Authority to leave
If you choose authority to leave, the courier may leave the parcel at your address without a signature. Responsibility passes to you once the courier confirms delivery to the address you gave us.

## Check your address
Please check your delivery details at checkout. If a parcel is returned because the address was incorrect or incomplete, we may charge the cost of sending it again. Contact us straight away if you notice a mistake.

## Damaged, lost or delayed parcels
If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph the packaging and contents and email sales@gtstreet.au as soon as you can. If a parcel appears lost or delayed, contact us and we will lodge and follow up the courier enquiry. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, including for goods that arrive damaged or are not delivered, are not affected.

## Risk and ownership
Risk passes to you on delivery to your nominated address. Ownership passes to you once we receive full payment.
