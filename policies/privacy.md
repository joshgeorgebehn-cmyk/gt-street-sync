# Privacy policy

GT Street Pty Ltd (ABN 82 692 485 868) ("GT Street", "we", "us") manages personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and how to contact us.

## What we collect
- **Contact details:** name, email, phone, billing and delivery address.
- **Order details:** what you view, add to cart, buy, return or ask about, and your vehicle details when you give them for fitment.
- **Payment details:** payments are processed by Shopify Payments, Shop Pay and Afterpay. We do not see or store your full card number.
- **Enquiries:** what you send through our forms, email, phone, Facebook or Instagram, including quote and finance enquiries.
- **GT Street Rewards:** your entries, results, prizes and the state of your delivery address, used to run the promotion.
- **Device and usage data:** IP address, browser, pages visited and similar data collected through cookies and analytics tools.

## How we collect it
Mostly directly from you when you browse, buy, enquire or sign in. Some comes from our service providers (for example payment providers and couriers) and from cookies on our site.

## Why we use it
- To process orders, check fitment, arrange delivery, handle returns and warranty claims, and provide customer support.
- To run promotions you take part in, including GT Street Rewards.
- To prevent fraud and keep our site secure.
- To send marketing, only where you have agreed to receive it. Every marketing email and SMS includes a way to unsubscribe, and we action requests within 5 business days. Order and account emails are not marketing and are still sent.
- To improve our site and range, using combined and de-identified data where we can.
- To meet our legal obligations.

If you do not give us the information we ask for, we may not be able to process your order or enquiry.

## Who we share it with
We share only what each provider needs to do its job:
- Shopify, which hosts our store, and the apps that run on it (for example our reviews and email tools).
- Payment providers: Shopify Payments, Shop Pay and Afterpay.
- Couriers, and suppliers who ship products directly to you.
- Finance brokers, only when you ask us to refer you.
- Analytics and advertising providers such as Google and Meta, through cookies.
- Professional advisers, and government bodies where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information.

## Overseas disclosure
Some providers store or process information outside Australia. Shopify and its apps, and Google and Meta, are likely to process information in countries including Canada, the United States and Ireland. We take reasonable steps to make sure these providers protect your information. The GT Street Rewards system is run by us in Australia.

## Automated decisions
GT Street Rewards results are decided by a computer program before each entry is revealed, using random allocation that treats every entry the same way. Our payment providers also use automated tools to screen orders for fraud. If you have a question about an automated decision, contact us and a person will review it.

## Cookies
Our site uses cookies to keep your cart, remember preferences and measure how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser; some features may not work without them.

## Security and retention
We use secure, access controlled systems and limit access to staff and providers who need it. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires (for example, tax and promotion records), then delete or de-identify it.

## Access, correction and deletion
You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we do not need to keep it. Email sales@gtstreet.au. We respond within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first. There is no charge for a request.

## Complaints
If you have a privacy concern, email sales@gtstreet.au with the details. We will acknowledge it within 5 business days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au) or on 1300 363 992.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The current version and its date are always shown below.
