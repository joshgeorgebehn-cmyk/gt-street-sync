# Fitment guarantee (v1.0, drafted 23/11/2026, page /pages/fitment-guarantee, saved hidden pending Josh's review)

Our promise. If we confirm a wheel, exhaust or suspension part fits your car and it doesn't, we put it right at our cost. You choose an exchange for the right part or a full refund, and we pay the freight both ways.

1. Tell us your car. Give us the year, make, model and variant, plus anything that changes fitment: upgraded brakes, aftermarket suspension or ride height, guard or body work, spacers, and your current wheel and tyre sizes. Add it at checkout, in My garage or by email.
2. We check before it ships. For wheels we check PCD, centre bore, offset, width and brake clearance. For exhausts and suspension we check the vehicle, engine and body application. If anything is unclear, we contact you before we send it.
3. Trial fit first. Offer wheels up to the car before tyres are mounted: they should sit flat on the hub, clear the brake calipers and take the studs or bolts properly. Have exhausts and suspension trial fitted by a qualified installer before any cutting, welding or final installation.
4. Tell us within 14 days. If it doesn't fit, email support@gtstreet.au within 14 days of delivery with your order number and photos showing the problem. We reply within two business days.

## What we do
- We arrange and pay for the return freight.
- Exchange: we send the correct part and pay the delivery. If it costs more or less than the original, we charge or refund the difference.
- Refund: we refund the full price of the part and your original delivery charge to your original payment method within 10 business days of receiving it back.

## Conditions
- We confirmed fitment for the vehicle you described, before dispatch.
- The part has not been driven on, cut, welded, painted or otherwise modified, and wheels have not had tyres mounted. Light marks from a careful trial fit are fine.
- It comes back with its original packaging and hardware, in a condition we can resell.
- This applies to special order and quoted parts too, as long as we confirmed fitment in writing.

## Not covered
- Vehicle details that were missing or incorrect, or changes to the car you didn't tell us about.
- Tyre sizes we did not recommend, rubbing caused by tyres, and ride height changes made after purchase.
- Parts bought against our written advice, or where we told you fitment could not be confirmed.
- Deciding you don't like how it looks. That is a change of mind return under our Returns policy.
- Fitting, labour and wheel alignment costs are not covered by this guarantee, but you may be entitled to compensation for reasonably foreseeable loss under the Australian Consumer Law.

## Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law
Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

This fitment guarantee is in addition to those rights and to the manufacturer's warranty. It does not limit them. Faulty parts are handled under our Returns policy and the Australian Consumer Law.
